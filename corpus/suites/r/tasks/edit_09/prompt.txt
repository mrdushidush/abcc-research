EDIT-09 -- a diff body line whose content begins with `--- ` or `+++ ` is silently discarded.

THE FILE, and the only one you may change: `crates/claudette/src/tools/patch.rs` (635 lines). It
already has a `#[cfg(test)] mod tests` at the bottom, opening with `use super::*;`, to put your
test in.

THE DEFECT, measured on this repo. `parse_diff` (line 164) walks the diff line by line (line 185)
and checks `line.starts_with("+++ ")` (line 186) and `line.starts_with("--- ")` (line 200) BEFORE
it looks at whether it is inside a hunk. So:

* an ADDED line whose text is `++ added` arrives as `+++ added` and is taken for a new-file
  header: the line vanishes from the hunk, and the patch still applies, `ok:true`;
* a DELETED line whose text is `-- ` (an email signature delimiter) arrives as `--- ` and is
  taken for an old-file header.

Measured: this diff against `"a\nb\n"`

    --- a/f.txt
    +++ b/f.txt
    @@ -1,2 +1,3 @@
     a
    +++ added
     b

should produce `"a\n++ added\nb\n"`. Today the added line is lost.

THE FIX -- the hunk header already says how long the body is. `@@ -L,N +L,M @@` promises N
old-side lines (context + deletions) and M new-side lines (context + additions); a missing count
means 1. While the current hunk still owes body lines on either side, every line is body -- a
`+`-line is an addition and a `-`-line a deletion whatever follows the marker. Only once the counts
are used up (or outside any hunk) are `--- ` / `+++ ` / `@@ ` headers.

1. Parse N and M from the header along with L (today only L is read, lines 216-230).
2. Keep a running count of the body lines each side still owes; decrement it as context (both
   sides), deletions (old side) and additions (new side) arrive.
3. Test for the three headers only when the current hunk owes nothing. Remove the two
   "defensive" `continue`s inside the body match (lines 245-249 and 253-255) that drop such lines.

A multi-file diff must still split into its files: after `@@ -1 +1 @@`, `-old`, `+new`, the next
`--- a/y.txt` / `+++ b/y.txt` IS a header.

Keep the signatures of `parse_diff` and `apply_hunks`, and the fields of `Hunk` and `FileDiff`,
exactly as they are -- other code and tests call them.

A TEST, in the `mod tests` that is already there: parse the diff above, apply its hunks to
`"a\nb\n"` with `apply_hunks`, and assert `"a\n++ added\nb\n"`. It fails on the current code.
